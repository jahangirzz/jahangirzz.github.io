SmartGoal app

iOS and Android app for moving motorized sports goals around a field

I joined while the app was an early prototype and turned it into a working product that sports facilities can use on game day, with real accounts, teams and live field control.

  • Accounts: sign-up, sign-in and account deletion, built to App Store requirements.

Personal project · 2026

Student Assistant

A personal assistant that connects to Canvas and my email and plans my day

I wanted one place that told me what to work on first, without opening every course. Student Assistant runs on my laptop, reads every class from Canvas, and emails me a plan.

  • Canvas sync: pulls assignments, quizzes, discussions and events from the Canvas calendar feed, or from the Canvas API when a token is available (adds submission status and announcements).
  • Daily briefing: a 7 a.m. email with what to do first and why, what's due, and warnings for heavy days, plus a Sunday evening preview of the week.
  • Alerts: heads-up emails when work is due within 24 hours or 3 hours, is overdue, or is newly posted, with Windows and phone push notifications. Quiet hours run from 11 p.m. to 7 a.m. except for urgent items.
  • Dashboard: a local web page with what's due, a calendar and a focus timer.
  • Private by design: uses only the Python standard library, and none of my data goes to an outside AI service.
